# VIDEO SCRIPT — under 3 minutes, hard limit

Format: screen recording of the LIVE app (greenreceipt-hazel.vercel.app), your
voice over it. Face-cam optional for the first 15 seconds only. This script is
~400 words — about 2:45 at normal pace, leaving real slack. Rehearse once
against a timer. Two takes maximum; done beats perfect.

Recording checklist:
- 1080p capture, every other tab closed, browser at 100% zoom, notifications off
- Sign in as **Managing Director** from the demo dropdown (only the MD can anchor)
- Click **Reset demo data** on the Upload page — clean seed, clean demo
- `demo_assets/S_payroll_headcount_2025_FIXED.xlsx` sitting on the desktop
- Do the anchor once in the morning so you have a fallback signature if devnet sulks

---

**[0:00–0:20 — THE PROBLEM. Face cam or title slide.]**

"A small factory in Shah Alam gets an email from its biggest customer: 'Complete
this ESG questionnaire. Two weeks.' They have no sustainability team. Their ESG
department is one very stressed HR manager and a folder called 'Misc'. The
evidence exists — it's just hiding in TNB bills, a payroll Excel, and a policy
nobody has opened since 2019."

**[0:20–0:40 — THE SOLUTION. Screen: Upload page, show the three files.]**

"This is EcoMetrics. You upload the documents you actually have — PDF bills,
Excel payroll, Word policy. AI reads the messy stuff — and that is ALL the AI
does. Every rule and every score after that is boring, predictable code. In ESG
reporting, boring is a feature."

**[0:40–1:20 — THE DASHBOARD. Screen: charts, then Governance card.]**

"Here's the honest map. Electricity — every month's number printed on the chart.
One month is only an estimate, three months say 'No bill'. Missing is not zero,
and this app refuses to pretend otherwise.

Now, Governance. This company HAS an anti-bribery policy — a checkbox tool would
mark that green. We actually read it: it's a draft, nobody signed it, the review
was due six years ago, and it references a gift register that does not exist.
That's not a policy, that's fan fiction. Red."

**[1:20–1:55 — PRIORITIES. Screen: legend, scores, create action on E-GAP.]**

"Every problem is scored and the math is printed on the card — severity three,
customer asked, quick win: nine point zero. No vibes. An auditor can check our
ranking in four seconds. And every finding arrives with the next step already
written. Top of the list: two workers below Malaysia's RM1,700 minimum wage.
Nobody knew. The spreadsheet knew. One click — it's an action: owner, deadline,
next step, all pre-filled. Oh, and even our intern gets a login. He can view and
upload. He cannot touch. We love him."

**[1:55–2:25 — THE MOMENT. Screen: upload the FIXED payroll.]**

"HR fixes the salaries, re-uploads the file. Watch. The system re-reads
everything, re-runs all twelve rules — and closes the action by itself.
'Resolved — verified by re-scan.' Nobody clicked done. The data clicked done.
Social score: sixty-three to seventy-two. Everything still broken stays open.
No participation trophies."

**[2:25–2:50 — SOLANA. Screen: anchor, explorer, then break it.]**

"Finally, the Managing Director — and only the MD — signs off by anchoring the
report's fingerprint on Solana. Yes, blockchain. No, not like that. No coins, no
monkeys. Just a hash. Now change one number and verify again… MISMATCH. Caught.
That's the honest use of a blockchain."

**[2:50–3:00 — CLOSE.]**

"From messy documents to proven, tracked action, in minutes — for the SMEs who
could never afford an ESG consultant. EcoMetrics. Thank you."

## Delivery notes

- Three beats that need a one-second pause: after "fan fiction. Red." — after
  "The data clicked done." — after "MISMATCH. Caught."
- The jokes work at normal speed. Do not slow down to signal 'this is the funny
  part' — deadpan lands better.
- Say "EcoMetrics" exactly twice: at 0:20 and at the close.
- If a live click misbehaves, cut, reload, redo that segment. Never narrate
  around a broken screen.
- Running over 3:00? Safest cuts, in order: "We love him." · the "No
  participation trophies" line · the "folder called 'Misc'" clause. Cut jokes
  before you cut evidence.
