# VIDEO SCRIPT — 3 minutes, hard limit

Format: screen recording of the DEPLOYED app, your voice over it. Face-cam intro
optional for the first 15 seconds only. Speak at a normal pace — this script is
~420 words, which lands at about 2:50, leaving 10 seconds of slack. Rehearse once
against a timer before recording. Two takes maximum; done beats perfect.

Recording checklist: 1080p screen capture, close every other tab, browser at 100%
zoom, notifications off, seed data loaded, FIXED payroll file on the desktop,
anchor signature from the morning ready as fallback.

---

**[0:00–0:25 — THE PROBLEM. Face cam or title slide.]**

"A small Malaysian factory gets an ESG questionnaire from its biggest customer.
Two weeks to respond, or risk the contract. No sustainability team. The evidence
exists — but it's scattered across electricity bills, an HR spreadsheet, and a
policy document nobody has opened in years.

Plenty of tools calculate carbon footprints. That was never the hard part. The hard
part is: what can we actually *prove*, and what do we do next?"

**[0:25–0:50 — THE SOLUTION. Screen: upload page, drop the three files.]**

"This is [APP NAME]. You upload the documents you actually have — a PDF of bills,
an Excel payroll file, a Word policy. AI reads the messy ones. Deterministic code
does everything else: every fact is stored with its source, and tagged as verified,
estimated, or missing."

**[0:50–1:30 — THE DASHBOARD. Screen: traffic lights, then click into Governance.]**

"Here's the company's real position. Not a score out of 100 — an honest map.
Environment: nine months of bills verified, one month is only an estimate, three
months are missing entirely. The system won't let an estimated number pretend to
be a fact.

And here's the part most tools get wrong. This company *has* an anti-bribery
policy — a checkbox tool would mark that green. Ours read it: it's a draft, never
signed, six years past its review date, and it references a gift register that
doesn't exist. A policy nobody adopted is not evidence. Amber, not green."

**[1:30–2:05 — PRIORITIES AND ACTION. Screen: priorities page, create the action.]**

"Every issue is scored — and the arithmetic is printed right there. Severity three,
the customer asked for it, fixable within the deadline: nine point zero. A judge, a
boss, or an auditor can check our ranking in four seconds.

Top of the list: two workers paid below Malaysia's RM1,700 minimum wage. Nobody
knew — it was sitting in a spreadsheet no one had read. One click: it becomes an
action, assigned to Siti in HR, due in three days — before the customer's deadline,
by construction."

**[2:05–2:35 — THE MOMENT. Screen: re-upload the fixed payroll file.]**

"HR fixes the salaries and re-uploads the file. Watch the top item. The system
re-reads the data, re-runs every rule — and closes the action itself: resolved,
verified by re-scan. Nobody clicked 'done'. The data proved it. Everything still
broken stays open."

**[2:35–2:55 — SOLANA. Screen: anchor button, explorer link.]**

"Finally — the report is hashed and anchored on Solana. If anyone edits a single
number afterwards, verification fails. Tamper-evident ESG reporting: the honest
use of a blockchain."

**[2:55–3:00 — CLOSE.]**

"From scattered documents to proven, tracked action — in minutes, for companies
that could never afford an ESG team. Thank you."

---

## Delivery notes

- The two pauses that matter: after "Amber, not green." and after "The data proved
  it." Let each breathe for one second.
- If a live click misbehaves during recording, cut, reload, redo that segment —
  don't narrate around a broken screen.
- Say the app's real name at 0:25 and again at the close.
- Do NOT go over 3:00. If your first take lands at 3:05, cut two sentences from
  the 0:50 section (the "Not a score out of 100" line and "Ours read it" clause
  are the safest cuts).
